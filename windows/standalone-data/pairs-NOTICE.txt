pairs.txt — word-pair statistics for the Tingzi Zhuyin engine

Each line is "first<TAB>second<TAB>bonus": two words from the word list that occur next to each
other more often than their separate frequencies predict, and by how many powers of ten.

The numbers were counted from the text of Chinese Wikipedia (zh.wikipedia.org), the partial
article dump "zhwiki-latest-pages-articles1", by the script windows/tools/build_pairs.py in the
Tingzi source repository. Only counts were kept; no article text is included.
